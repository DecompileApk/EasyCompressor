EasyCompressor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

EasyCompressor is developed by junlangliuqiao. It compresses, resizes, converts and trims videos on your Android device. Video processing works offline and does not require an account.

1. On-device processing and local data

EasyCompressor reads the videos you select or share with it, including their audio and file details such as name, size, duration and resolution. It uses this information to preview, process and save your videos.

Processing takes place on your device. Temporary video copies, previews, recent task records and settings are stored in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app storage controls. Saved videos are placed in your device’s shared video storage or a folder you authorize.

The app does not automatically send your videos, audio, file details, processing history or device information to us. It includes no advertising, usage analytics or remote crash-reporting services. We do not sell your personal data.

2. File access and permissions

You choose which videos to open through Android’s picker or another app’s Share action. EasyCompressor may retain access to selected files, or keep a private copy, so a task can finish or be retried. If you select a save folder, access is used to save and manage the files created there. The app does not request unrestricted access to all files on your device.

Notifications show processing progress and let you cancel a task. A foreground service and a temporary wake lock help processing continue when you switch apps or the screen turns off. These permissions support video processing and are not used to collect personal data.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Audio already present in a selected video is processed as part of that video. You can manage notification and file-access permissions through Android’s settings and system controls.

3. Sharing and third-party services

When you choose Share video, Android’s share panel lets you select a receiving app. The selected app receives access to the video you share and may upload or otherwise process it under its own privacy policy.

If you choose a file or destination provided by a cloud storage app, that provider may transfer the file under its own privacy policy. EasyCompressor performs the video conversion on your device and does not operate a cloud processing service.

If you install or update EasyCompressor through Google Play, Google handles store-related information under its Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

Recent task records remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app’s storage. Removing a record alone does not delete the saved video. Choosing Delete file deletes the selected saved output. Processing and these deletion actions do not modify or delete your original video.

You can clear temporary files in the app’s settings. Clearing temporary files does not delete saved videos; copies still needed for unfinished tasks may be retained. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s private data. Videos saved in shared storage or another folder remain until you delete them using the app or your device’s file tools.

We do not hold copies of your videos or processing history on our servers. Copies shared with other apps or cloud providers must be managed with those services.

5. Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email junlangliuqiao@gmail.com.

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request, retain them only as long as needed for support or applicable legal obligations, and accept deletion requests at the same address.
